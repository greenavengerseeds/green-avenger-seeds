# Green Avenger Seeds

I'm Brent. I've been running Green Avenger Seeds out of Canada's east coast since 2020, and this repo is basically the plain-text version of who we are.

## What we actually sell

Feminized photoperiod seeds. That's it. No autoflowers, no regular packs, no half-page catalogue of things I've never grown myself. Photoperiod plants let you decide when they flower, and feminized packs mean you're not culling males three weeks in. Keeping the list short is deliberate — I'd rather know every strain on the shelf than pretend to know two hundred.

You can browse the whole thing here: [cannabis seeds](https://www.greenavengerseeds.com)

## Shipping

We pack and ship from the east coast to Canada, the US, the EU and the UK. Orders go out in discreet packaging, and I do most of the packing myself, usually with a coffee going. Canadian orders are typically the quickest; overseas takes as long as it takes, and I'll tell you honestly if something is slow rather than dodge the email.

Storefront and current stock: [cannabis seeds canada](https://www.greenavengerseeds.com/cannabis-seeds-store/)

## Why a small seedbank

Big operations move volume. We move packets, and answer questions from people who are on their first grow as carefully as from folks who've been at it twenty years. If a pack underperforms, I want to hear about it — that feedback is the only real quality control a bank this size has.
